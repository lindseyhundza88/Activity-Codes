# Activity Code Task Name & When to Use
001 SLS_Paralegal_001
Initial Case Review Case Work
Review of intake or new matter information. Includes SOL Setting, Injury Tier, Intake and review of client information based on Intake. May also include gathering and inputting initial information in Litify, and review completeness of intake or new matter record.
⚠ DO NOT use Data Clean Up for initial case review — log it here instead.
002 SLS_Paralegal_002
Client Communication Comms
Response to INBOUND client communication ONLY. Includes time on the phone with a client and time reviewing and responding to client emails. Use when a client call is in response to M&M Case Staff outreach (e.g. VM, text) OR when unprompted.
⚠ SHOULD NOT BE USED FOR OUTBOUND OUTREACH. For outbound, select the code matching the underlying reason for the call (e.g. CQ Retrieval, Medical Record Request). THIS IS SO WE HAVE VISIBILITY INTO INBOUND CLIENT CALLS.
003 SLS_Paralegal_003
Case Audit Case Work
General review of matter information (excluding initial case review) to identify missing information / documents; validate client injuries and tiers, proof of injury, proof of usage, assess settlement readiness, evaluate client for Turn Down, etc.
004 SLS_Paralegal_004
CQ Retrieval Case Work
Outreach to clients related to CQ or HIPAA retrieval. Includes BOTH outreach to clients with no CQ on file AND outreach to obtain missing or updated information or other documents.
005 SLS_Paralegal_005
CQ Review Case Work
Review of submitted Questionnaire, Validation of Client info, Setting SOLs based on CQ. Includes updating Litify Matters with information from CQ, whether received electronically or via Docufree.
006 SLS_Paralegal_006
Medical Record Request/Follow Up Medical
Submission of MR record requests via Litify. Creation of roles, other information in Litify required to submit requests. Includes client OUTREACH and other activities to gather additional information required to request medical records (e.g. SF180 or DD2870 forms) beyond HIPAA and CQ.
007 SLS_Paralegal_007
Medical Record Review Medical
Review of Medical Records, updating the Medical History tab in Litify, creation of Medical record summaries, review of other documents required to validate and prove usage / exposure and injury of client.
008 SLS_Paralegal_008
Complaint Prep/Draft/Review Docs & Filings
Preparation of complaints, including filling in forms as well as reaching out to clients and/or running Locates to gather required information.
009 SLS_Paralegal_009
Complaint Filing/Service Docs & Filings
Filing or submitting to portal complaints and other required court submissions (e.g. census form, tolling agreement, PFS, Settlement package, Proof of Claim, etc.).
010 SLS_Paralegal_010
PFS Prep/Draft/Review Docs & Filings
Preparation of PFS docs, including filling in forms, correcting deficiencies, and reaching out to clients and/or running Locates to gather required information.
011 SLS_Paralegal_011
PFS Submission/Service Docs & Filings
Submission or service of completed PFS documents to the appropriate portal or party.
012 SLS_Paralegal_012
Tolling/Census Doc Prep/Review Docs & Filings
Preparation of tolling agreements, census forms, including filling in forms as well as reaching out to clients and/or running Locates to gather required information.
013 SLS_Paralegal_013
Misc. Prep/Draft/Review Docs & Filings
Preparation of court submissions other than PFS or Census Forms, including filling in forms, correcting deficiencies, and reaching out to clients and/or running Locates. Example: Drafting/reviewing/filing Proof of Claim (POC) forms for Defendant bankruptcy filings.
014 SLS_Paralegal_014
Misc. Filings/Submissions Docs & Filings
Filing or submitting to portal complaints and other required court submissions (e.g. census form, tolling agreement, PFS, Settlement package, Proof of Claim, etc.).
015 SLS_Paralegal_015
Bellwether Identification Case Work
Identification and evaluation of matters and matter information for selection as a potential bellwether candidate.
016 SLS_Paralegal_016
TD Processing Admin
Sending client TD letter, processing Finance request to close matter that has been turned down.
017 SLS_Paralegal_017
Data Clean Up Admin
Update of information in Litify or Spreadsheets. Includes updates to Display Names, Statuses, Questionnaire Status, Medical records status, Injury Status, File #, and other Litify fields, etc.
⚠ DO NOT USE when conducting initial case review — log that under Initial Case Review (001).
018 SLS_Paralegal_018
Case Status Mtgs/Calls with Leads/Team Comms
Check-ins with Team Leads/Case Ops Managers or weekly calls with Attorney/Lead Paralegal related to case work up, updates, etc.
019 SLS_Paralegal_019
Litigation Support/Prep Case Work
Activities related to support for bellwether trials, including PHV (pro hac vice), Rule 707 discovery, deposition-prep, Review ECF Notifications, Science Committee related activities, etc.
⚠ REACH OUT TO LEAD ATTORNEY OR NEFF BEFORE SELECTING THIS ACTIVITY.
020 SLS_Paralegal_020
NOK Research/Outreach Comms
Identification of and outreach to NOK to serve as representative of client. Includes running locates, obtaining death certificates, and projects related to determining date of death.
021 SLS_Paralegal_021
Probate/Estate/Trust Case Work
Outreach to and working with clients, third parties, or other firms regarding estates and probate.
⚠ DOES NOT include outreach to potential NOKs or research to determine legal NOK.
022 SLS_Paralegal_022
Case Value/Enhancement Research Case Work
Tort-specific projects to gather information or evidence that could strengthen or increase the potential value of a client's claim.
023 SLS_Paralegal_023
Settlement Prep/Draft/Review Settlement
Preparation of settlement packages, including submitting forms/data to settlement portal and curing deficiencies. INCLUDES outreach to clients to retrieve and complete forms needed to participate in a settlement (e.g. governmental and private liens, personal bankruptcy, claim forms). Includes running locates as part of settlement package prep process.
024 SLS_Paralegal_024
Settlement Distribution Settlement
Preparation of Case Closing statements and activities related to disbursing funds to clients. Includes communication with clients regarding status of settlement payment and details of Closing Statement, as well as running Locates as part of disbursement process.
025 SLS_Paralegal_025
Finance Admin
Internal activity related to Finance queries, billing statements, expense allocations, attorney reimbursements, etc. Includes internal communications with finance team as well as usage of Litify. Includes external communications with QSF (if applicable).
⚠ DOES NOT include medical record invoicing approval. DOES NOT include preparation of client settlement distributions, closing statements nor related communication with clients.
026 SLS_Paralegal_026
Admin Checking Emails & Docufree Admin
Reviewing inbox, checking / processing Docufree.
027 SLS_Paralegal_027
Admin Monthly Meeting/Training Admin
Attending internal M&M meetings that are not directly associated with your assigned Tort, such as org meetings and trainings.
028 SLS_Paralegal_028
Admin Organizing/Prioritizing Tasks Admin
Planning your day, entering time in Bill4Time, prioritizing tasks, preparing to-do lists, etc.
029 SLS_Paralegal_029
Admin Tech Issues Admin
Time spent dealing with M&M IT to resolve tech issues, e.g. related to remote desktop, log-in access, Litify bugs, etc.
